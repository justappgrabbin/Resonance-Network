# Donor provenance

The integration preserves the original donor archives unchanged. Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `DONOR_SHA256.txt`.

## Chassis

### Creator-Resonance.zip
Used as the main application chassis because it already contained the React/Vite client, Express service, PostgreSQL/Drizzle persistence, authentication, Notebook IDE, resonance profile, Paper Seed organism, and local consciousness service integration.

## Donor roles

### Kimi_Agent_Consciousness Lab Engine.zip
Research/lab and agent-consciousness donor. Concepts and services are integrated only where they can sit behind explicit interfaces. Research code is not automatically treated as validated evidence.

### SynthAi-Proto-mobile-main.zip
Orchestration, graph/morphing, material-ingestion, simulation, local intelligence, and mobile-oriented Synthia donor. Its internal Synthia mechanisms remain conceptually distinct from generic coding-worker swarms.

### GlyphResonance.zip
Creator/file/glyph and project-assembly donor. Useful for project lineage and creator-tooling direction.

### Resonance-Network-main (1).zip and ResonanceNetwork.zip
Earlier network/profile/revenue/bootstrap donors. Their role is historical continuity and feature recovery, not replacement of the current chassis.

### resonance_social_mvp_20250831_034011 (1)(3).zip
Earlier social-network donor. Used as product lineage reference rather than as the primary shell.

### valence-chemistry-starter (1).zip
Relationship/valence mechanics donor for future compositional matching and living-system rules.

## Canonical product intent

The uploaded Resonance Network and Pathways to Purpose documents define the product direction: personal AI companion onboarding, individualized roadmaps, purpose-driven social collaboration, project nodes, solo and collaborative quests, science and enterprise routes, creator launch tooling, real-world field projects, contextual matching, and useful outcomes.

The integration follows that product contract rather than choosing donors based only on code volume.
