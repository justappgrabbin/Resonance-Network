# North Star product contract

The Resonance Network exists to help people turn direction, unfinished work, skills, ideas, experiments, and needs into useful real-world outcomes with the right people.

## Non-negotiable product spine

### 1. Personal companion + onboarding
Every user has a Synthia companion. Onboarding learns who the person is, what they want, what they are trying to change, where they are stuck, what resources they have, and what they can contribute. Human Design and resonance mechanics can enrich that context when supplied.

### 2. Pathways to Purpose
The app produces a living roadmap, not a static report. A current optimal vector should always be visible and update as circumstances and evidence change.

### 3. People and roles
Connections should be explainable in terms of a live project need, complementary strengths, shared goals, context, and capacity. Need-in-Proximity and calendar/location context remain opt-in.

### 4. Missions and quests
The system turns direction into bounded action. Missions carry an objective, role, rationale, actions, evidence, and completion state. Solo and collaborative missions both belong.

### 5. Project nodes
A project is a network object. It can be joined, commented on, remixed, replicated, funded, supported, and moved between research and product pathways.

### 6. Resonance: Science
Speculative or unconventional ideas can be turned into explicit hypotheses, methods, variables, observables, logs, and replication attempts. The interface must keep hypothesis, observation, evidence, and interpretation distinct.

### 7. Resonance: Enterprise
Useful work can become products, services, jobs, collaborations, launches, funding requests, and sustainable income opportunities. Enterprise remains connected to its source project and outcomes.

### 8. Creator Launcher / Paper lineage
Creators can bring ZIPs or repositories into the system, preserve source provenance, make the work legible, build/launch it, and promote the result into a project node.

### 9. Real-world feedback
The network should care about what happened after the recommendation. Missions, matches, launches, experiments, and opportunities feed evidence back into future guidance.

### 10. YOU-N-I-VERSE / IndiVerse
Worlds are modular shared contexts. Each host world keeps its own rules. Visiting Synths adapt to the host instead of forcing the world to conform to them. Identity and source lineage remain portable.

## System boundary

Synthia's living/life-process swarm is not a generic code-cleanup worker pool. External engineering swarms may repair, test, assemble, or maintain the repository around Synthia through normal software interfaces without repurposing her internal swarm.

## Definition of a successful build

A person should be able to enter the application and move through this chain without leaving the product:

`onboard → understand current direction → find a useful person or mission → create/join a project → test or commercialize it → build/launch it → record what happened → receive the next vector`

A build that only presents dashboards, profiles, or chat without this movement does not satisfy the North Star.
