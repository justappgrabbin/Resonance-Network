How Paper Works

From concept to live site in four simple steps

1

Describe Your Vision

Tell Paper what you want to build in plain English. Describe your goals, your audience, the feeling you want to create, and any specific features you need. There is no coding required and no technical jargon necessary. Just explain your vision as if you were talking to a colleague who understands what you are trying to achieve.

2

AI Generates Your Site

Paper's artificial intelligence transforms your description into a complete website architecture, generates the design system, creates all necessary components, and even produces immersive visual environments through Vibes if your concept calls for rich interactive experiences. The entire generation process typically completes in under thirty seconds.

3

Review and Refine

Your generated site appears instantly for review. Use natural language to request any changes you would like to see. Want different colors, alternative layouts, or additional features? Simply describe what you want adjusted and Paper implements the changes immediately. The refinement process is conversational and iterative until everything matches your vision perfectly.

4

Deploy and Evolve

Click deploy and your site goes live instantly with automatic SSL certificates, global content delivery network distribution, and mobile responsiveness already configured. Then watch as Paper Seed learns from how people interact with your site and suggests improvements over time. Your website continues evolving long after the initial launch, growing more effective as it learns what works best for your specific audience and goals.

Common Questions

How is Paper fundamentally different from traditional website builders like Wix or Webflow? +
Paper represents a paradigm shift in how websites are created and maintained. Traditional builders require you to manually drag widgets, configure layouts, and manage every detail yourself. Paper uses artificial intelligence and evolutionary algorithms to understand what you want to build and generates it for you based on natural language descriptions. More importantly, with Paper Seed, your site continues evolving after launch by learning from user behavior and suggesting improvements autonomously. Vibes adds another revolutionary layer by generating immersive visual environments where interaction emerges from the imagery itself rather than being overlaid as separate UI components.
What exactly does Vibes do that regular image backgrounds cannot accomplish? +
Traditional websites place interactive elements like buttons and links on top of static images or videos as separate layers. Vibes generates environments where the visual elements themselves are the interactive surfaces. When you create a castle door that opens to navigate to another section, the door opening animation and the navigation action are unified rather than coordinated through scripting. This creates fundamentally more immersive experiences because users interact with the world itself rather than with UI controls floating above it. The technology bridges generative AI with spatial interaction semantics in ways that existing tools simply cannot replicate.
Can I export my code and host the site on my own infrastructure? +
Yes, absolutely. Pro and Team plans include full source code export capabilities. You can download your entire site as clean, production-ready code and host it anywhere you prefer. You maintain complete ownership of everything Paper generates for you. The exported code is standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript without any proprietary dependencies, so you can modify it directly or integrate it with your existing development workflow if you choose.
What does self-evolving actually mean in practical terms? +
Paper Seed and Papermate employ machine learning to analyze your content structure, user interaction patterns, and successful design elements. Paper Seed suggests new features based on what your audience needs, optimizes layouts according to engagement data, and identifies opportunities for growth that you might not have considered. Papermate takes this further by cross-breeding successful elements from different applications through genetic algorithms, creating hybrid variations that combine the best characteristics of multiple sources. Natural selection principles ensure only the most effective combinations survive and propagate, while emergent behaviors sometimes produce capabilities that were not present in either parent application.
Is there truly a free plan with no catch? +
Yes, our Starter plan is genuinely free forever with no credit card required. You get three Paper sites, one Paper Seed project, access to basic Cardstock templates, and standard Vibes features. This is not a limited trial that expires. We built Paper because we believe revolutionary technology should be accessible to everyone, and we want you to experience the platform without any financial commitment.
